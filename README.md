### 🌱 A little about my work

I build machine learning systems that don't just predict — they explain themselves. My focus sits at the intersection of applied ML and interpretability: models that hold up under scrutiny in regulated, high-stakes settings like finance. Outside of modeling, I care about systems thinking applied to teams and communities, which is what pulled me toward leadership programs alongside my technical work.

### 🧪 Featured builds

<details open>
<summary><b>🏦 FinGuard AI — ML Decision Engine for Banking</b></summary>
<br>

A unified pipeline covering credit risk scoring, fraud detection, churn prediction, and spend forecasting in one system, evaluated on business-driven metrics (ROC-AUC, F1, RMSE, R²) with SHAP-based explainability baked in for regulated environments.

`Python` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `MongoDB`
📌 Showcased at Sukkur IBA as an Applied ML Benchmark

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🧩 Maze Adventure — Algorithmic Game Engine</b></summary>
<br>

A Java-based game with live BFS/DFS enemy navigation, DFS-generated mazes, and full MySQL persistence for player lives, state, and leaderboards.

`Java` `Swing` `BFS/DFS` `Dijkstra` `MySQL`
📌 Recognized at AI & CS Expo 2024

</details>

`Java` `Swing` `MySQL`
